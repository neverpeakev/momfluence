The route returned 200 with no log output, meaning it exited via one of three silent early-return paths in `app/api/social/ig-mirror/route.ts`:
1. `META_MARKETING_API_TOKEN or META_FB_PAGE_ID not set` (line 154–162)
2. `nothing to mirror` (line 166–173)
3. `auth/page-context: <err>` (line 184–192)

Path 2 is ruled out — the row matches `listPendingIgMirror`'s filter exactly (status `fb_published`, fb_published_at set, ig_published_at null).
