## Root cause
`fetchPageContext` in `app/api/social/ig-mirror/route.ts` fails with `No Instagram Business Account linked to FB Page 1086292991223812` because the IG Business Account was unlinked from the FB Page on ~2026-05-28 and has not been reconnected. The mirror catches that error at line 182–192, returns HTTP 200 with `ok: false` and a message in the body, but **does not mark any pending rows as `ig_failed`**. So the row sits at `fb_published` with no error trail, and the next day's mirror picks it up alongside the new day's row.

Vercel runtime logs today confirm the flow:
- 14:15:08 UTC — `GET /api/social/ig-mirror` returns 200 in ~8s (too fast to reach the polling loop, matches the auth-fail path)
- No serverless-level logs (function emits none in the fail path)

This is why 2026-07-28, -29, -30 rows are all frozen at `fb_published` with `error_message = null`, and why on 2026-07-29 someone had to manually SQL-mark the 2026-07-18..27 backlog as `ig_failed` with a "skipped-stale-backlog" note to prevent a mass-publish once the IG link comes back.

## Fixes

### Manual (blocker — cannot be done in code)
Reconnect the Instagram Business Account (id `17841442248205840`) to the Momfluence FB Page (id `1086292991223812`) inside Meta Business Suite → Settings → Accounts → Instagram accounts → Connect. Once reconnected, the mirror will resume on the next 14:15 UTC firing for that day's fresh row only (older rows will remain `ig_failed` and are intentionally not backfilled).

### Code (PR)
Branch `claude/fix-content-cron-2026-07-31` — makes the mirror mark every pending row `ig_failed` when `fetchPageContext` throws, so the failure is recorded on the row itself and the health cron catches it the next day instead of two months later.
