level, but every single variant row shows **`visits=0`, `conversions=0`,

   *exist* in the account, and signups from other traffic sources not the
   funnel-lab variants).

`variant_events` (or the equivalent site-side visit table) directly, and
confirm whether the variant slug is arriving on landing-page hits.
