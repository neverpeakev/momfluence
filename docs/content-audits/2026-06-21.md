## TL;DR

The week is a **format-monoculture failure**. 7 of 7 posts are `anecdote`. 4 of 7 are set in Cincinnati. 5 of 7 share the same headline shape ("She [verb]ed [thing] / [reaction]"). 5 of 7 use `warm-gradient` for `image_bg`. The canonical voice is solid — vocabulary, eligibility puncture, CTA, dead-phrase absence are all clean. The system is producing **on-thesis posts in lockstep**, which is worse than off-thesis variety because it trains the audience that every Momfluence post is the same micro-story.

Root cause: the SYSTEM_PROMPT's first format example is `anecdote` and there's no explicit rotation directive. Without one, Claude picks the safest format every day.

## Pattern analysis

### 1. Format monoculture (CRITICAL)
7/7 anecdotes. Zero of the other four formats. This is the most important pattern of the week — the system is functionally producing one type of post.

### 2. Geographic monoculture
- Cincinnati: 4 (06-16, 06-17, 06-18, 06-19)
- Charlotte: 2 (06-15, 06-20)
- Asheville: 1 (06-21)

All Southern/Mid-South. No coastal, no big-city, no small-town. Reader pattern recognition kicks in fast.

### 3. Setting / scene monoculture
- School pickup line: 2 back-to-back (06-16, 06-17)
- School/kid-event-adjacent: 5 of 7 (16, 17, 18, 19, 20 — graduation, mascara at school auction, swim lessons, pickup, pickup)
- Waiting room / urgent care / farmers market: 2 (only 06-15 and 06-21 break the school orbit)

### 4. Headline shape monoculture
"She [verb]ed [thing] / [reaction]" appears in 6 of 7:
- "She named the tote / before the tomatoes were bagged."
- "She told the row behind her / where the dress was from."
- "She named the goggles / before lap two."
- "She reapplied her mascara / and three moms wrote it down."
- "She named the car seat / through the rolled-down window."
- "She rolled down her window / and named the costume."

Only 06-15 ("She told the coughing kid's mom / which humidifier actually works.") varies the verb slightly, but the shape is the same.

### 5. Image_bg monoculture
- `warm-gradient`: 5 of 7
- `navy-coral-gradient`: 2 of 7
- `coral`, `navy`, `cream`, `white-coral-ring`: **0 each**

Visual feed is monochrome.

### 6. Vocabulary slip
"Products and services" appears in only 2 of 7 posts. The other 5 say "products," "exactly that," or "the stuff." The thesis explicitly says to use "products and services" because Hulu, HBO, Disney+ are services — and dropping the word narrows the implicit catalog in the reader's head.

### 7. Caption length
Average ~180 words. Trending toward the upper end of the 80–300 word target. Not a violation, but the long ones (06-21, 06-18) bury the CTA further down than ideal for FB skim-reading.

### 8. No new dead phrases slipped through
BLOCKLIST is doing its job. No "gate-kept," no "rev share," no "everyday moms," no pain-point openers, no "real talk." This is good.

### 9. $5/mo absence
$5/mo never appears in any of the 7 posts. Per thesis this is acceptable for organic, but a week-long absence is worth flagging — at least one "here's how it works" beat per week would naturally drop it in.
