## Pattern analysis

### 1. Format monoculture (SEVERE)

7 of 7 posts are `anecdote`. The v5 thesis explicitly says variety comes from format rotation, not from message variation. The system is failing the variety axis entirely. A reader who sees the Momfluence page 3× in a week sees the same texture 3×.

**Why it's happening:** the anecdote example in the SYSTEM_PROMPT is the most vivid and fully-fleshed of the five formats. Once Claude has locked in on the "she named the ___ in a specific place" template, it looks like the safest and most on-brand format. There's no explicit anti-monoculture instruction telling it to actively rotate.

### 2. Anecdote template overfit

Within the anecdote bucket, the writing has calcified into a single template:

- **Headline structure:** `"She named the ___ / at the ___."` — 6 of 7 posts (all except orchestra, which is close: "She named the rosin / at the orchestra concert").
- **Payout figures:** `$312` × 4, `$340` × 2, `$610` × 1. A reader watching the feed sees "$312" four times in seven days.
- **"Four moms bought it" beat:** 5 of 7 posts.
- **City reuse in a 7-day window:** Fort Wayne × 2, Sacramento × 2, plus Ohio / Grand Rapids / Tulsa.
- **Eyebrow:** "REAL MOM, REAL MONEY" (or plural / punctuation variant) × 6 of 7.

The individual anecdotes are well-written. The aggregate reads like Mad Libs.

### 3. Visual monoculture

`image_bg` is `warm-gradient` for **7 of 7 posts.** The renderer supports six backgrounds (coral, navy, cream, warm-gradient, navy-coral-gradient, white-coral-ring). The prompt says nothing about rotating them, so Claude picks its favorite every day.

### 4. Angle-tag naming pattern

Every angle_tag this week ends in `-rec` and follows `[venue]-[product]-rec`. That's fine as a naming convention but reinforces the format monoculture in the "recent angles" list fed back to Claude — every retry sees a wall of anecdote-shaped slugs and infers "anecdote is the norm."

### 5. Dead phrases

**Zero** dead-phrase hits. The BLOCKLIST is doing its job on the words it knows. Nothing to add.

### 6. Voice drift

None. The voice is locked and holding. "Her bow is doing that squeaky thing again" (08-08) is the smart-friend-texting register the thesis wants.

### 7. Caption length

All 130–200 words. Consistent, mobile-readable, no drift.

---

## Verdict — prompt tuning WARRANTED

The individual writing is good. The system-level pattern is not. This is a **variety/rotation** failure, not a voice or vocabulary failure.

### What the v6 tune does

1. **Force format rotation.** New `# FORMAT ROTATION (HARD RULE)` block in the SYSTEM_PROMPT: if the last several angle_tags/displays are the same format, MUST pick a different one. Explicit floor: no format can appear >2× in any 5-post window.
2. **Anecdote-specific guardrails.** New `# ANECDOTE GUARDRAILS` block: vary the payout figure meaningfully (not $312/$340 every time), vary the "N moms bought it" count, don't use the same city twice inside 10 posts, and do NOT structure every headline as `"She named the ___ at the ___."` — rotate other openings (dollar-first, quote-first, moment-first).
3. **Visual rotation.** New `# IMAGE VARIETY` block: rotate image_bg deliberately across the palette; do not default to `warm-gradient` every time.
4. **Headline anti-template.** Explicit note that the "She named the ___ at the ___" cadence is spent for now — needs to rest.
5. **Bump `PROMPT_VERSION` → `2026-08-09.v6`.**

### What the tune deliberately does NOT change

- The locked voice, canonical spine, or vocabulary tables — all still v5.
- The BLOCKLIST — no dead-phrase hits this week; no additions needed.
- The 3-piece message rule.
- The five content formats and their examples.
- The $5/mo policy.

This is a **refinement**, not a pivot. Not flagged `VOICE PIVOT PROPOSAL`.
