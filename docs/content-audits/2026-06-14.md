## 3. Pattern analysis

### 3a. Format monoculture (CRITICAL)

**Every single post this week was `anecdote`.** Zero `direct`, zero `math`, zero `brand-callout`, zero `objection-reframe`. The thesis explicitly designs the 5 formats as the variety axis — the message is locked, the texture is the variation. A full week of one texture violates this directly.

Root cause: the prompt shows the model `recentAngleTags` and `recentDisplays` but **never** shows it `recentContentFormats`. The model has no signal that it's been picking anecdote for 6 days straight. Format diversity is unenforceable without that signal.

### 3b. "A regular mom in [City]..." has become a template

4 of 6 posts open with that exact structural rhythm. Cincinnati used twice, Sacramento used twice in 7 days. The model has converged on a single anecdote template (third-person journalism lede → reveal → CTA) instead of using the locked casual openers ("Moms:", "Heads up moms:", "POV:", "Wait —") and then sliding into the anecdote.

This drifts the voice toward newsroom register — the exact thing the locked voice section forbids.

### 3c. Anecdote ≠ vignette

Per thesis (§ Content formats): anecdote requires *"a specific person, in a specific place, with a specific number."* Half of this week's anecdotes (06-14, 06-11, 06-10) have no dollar number. They're vignettes with brand-name sprinkling. The number IS the proof — without it, anecdote collapses into "story about a mom."

### 3d. Image_bg rut

5 of 6 used `warm-gradient`; 1 used `coral`. Zero use of `navy`, `cream`, `navy-coral-gradient`, `white-coral-ring`. The feed will start looking visually identical.

### 3e. Near-miss on dead-phrase pattern

Post 06-08: *"If you've ever sent a 'you NEED this' text, you already qualify."* — this is not a literal blocklist hit, but it's in the structural family of pain-point conditionals (`if you've ever wanted`, `wish you could`) that the thesis dead-lists. Acceptable this time (it's identity-confirming, not pain-pointing) but worth watching.

### 3f. No new dead phrases slipped through

The BLOCKLIST held. No "gate-kept," no "rev share," no "everyday moms," no "side hustle." This is good news.

## 4. Decision — prompt tuning warranted

The format monoculture (§3a) is severe enough to act on this week. The voice is fine, the canonical message is being delivered, the dead phrases are out — but the variety axis the thesis explicitly designed is collapsed.

Changes in this PR:
1. **`GeneratorInputs.recentContentFormats`** added (optional). When passed, the user prompt surfaces a count-by-format so the model sees the imbalance.
2. **`SYSTEM_PROMPT` — new section "FORMAT ROTATION"** — explicit rule: do not pick the same format as the most recent post; aim for distribution across all 5 over a 7-day window.
3. **`SYSTEM_PROMPT` — tightened anecdote definition** — anecdote MUST include a specific dollar number AND open with a locked casual opener (not a third-person journalism lede).
4. **`SYSTEM_PROMPT` — city rotation hint** — if a city appears in `recentAngleTags`, pick a different one.
5. **`SYSTEM_PROMPT` — image_bg rotation hint** — vary across the 6 options weekly.
6. **`PROMPT_VERSION`** bumped to `2026-06-14.v6` (operational refinement; v5 voice unchanged).
7. **`BLOCKLIST`** unchanged — no new dead phrases slipped through this week.

### Caller follow-up (out of scope for this PR per audit guardrails)

To make rule #1 enforceable end-to-end, the cron caller (likely `/api/social/fb-daily-generate`) needs to pass `recentContentFormats`. Until then, the rule lives in the system prompt as guidance but isn't grounded in actual data. Flagging for human reviewer to schedule.
