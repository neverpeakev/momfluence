## Pattern findings

### 1. Anecdote mono-culture (the headline finding)

Three of four v5-era posts (75%) chose `anecdote`. **Zero** `direct`, `math`, or
`objection-reframe`. The thesis says variety lives on the format axis — right
now it's living on the city/number axis instead. The model is defaulting to
the easiest format because it's the most concrete and the example in the
prompt is the most vivid.

### 2. Anecdote template-copy

All three anecdotes share near-identical structure:

| Post | Opener | City | Mom moment | Payout |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| #3 | "A regular mom in Cleveland sent her group chat..." | Cleveland | group chat leggings link | $340 |
| #4 | "A regular mom in Raleigh saw a Nextdoor post..." | Raleigh | Nextdoor stroller answer | $430 |
| #5 | "A regular mom in Sacramento was sitting in..." | Sacramento | preschool pickup diaper cream | $480 |

Posts 3 and 5 reuse the situation list **verbatim**: "in group chats, school
WhatsApps" appears in both. The cadence — "the [thing] you did [yesterday]
for free" — is a beat that's surfacing in every anecdote.

This is not yet a quality problem (these posts still score 5.0). It is a
**leading indicator** of AI-tells: when the template becomes visible,
readers stop reading.

### 3. Visual rut

Posts 3, 4, 5 all use `image_bg = warm-gradient`. Four of five accent badges
are dollar amounts (`$5`, `$340`, `$430`, `$480`). The anecdote format pulls
the same visual every time.

### 4. Structural gap in `GeneratorInputs`

`GeneratorInputs` surfaces `recentAngleTags` and `recentDisplays` to Claude
but **not** recent `content_format`s. The model has no way to see that it
just picked anecdote three days in a row. Adding `recentContentFormats` to
the prompt closes this loop.

### 5. Pre-v5 carry-over (post #1)

`gen-2026-05-13-mrocs` (created 01:26 UTC on 2026-05-13, before v5 rolled
out) uses `affiliate program`, `commission rate`, `commissions`, `affiliate
stuff`. Its `generation_metadata` lacks `content_format` and `rationale`,
confirming pre-v5 schema. Not a current-prompt failure, but flagged for
context. No BLOCKLIST changes needed — all those phrases are already on it.

### 6. Voice + canonical compliance (v5 posts) is solid

All four v5 posts deliver the full 3-piece canonical message. All four use
`regular moms`. All four end with `Find out more [and get yours/cut] at
momfluence.app.` The voice is consistent. The issue is variety, not voice.

## Prompt changes shipped this week (v5 → v6)

1. **New optional field** on `GeneratorInputs`: `recentContentFormats?:
   readonly string[]`. Backward-compatible — callers that don't pass it
   get the v5 behavior. Callers that do pass it surface the recent
   format rotation to Claude.
2. **New SYSTEM_PROMPT section: "FORMAT ROTATION"** — explicit rule that
   if a format appears 2+ times in the last 5 posts, pick a different
   one. Names the three undertested formats (`direct`, `math`,
   `objection-reframe`) so the model knows where to lean.
3. **Anecdote anti-template note** added next to the anecdote example.
   The "A regular mom in [City]..." opener is ONE shape, not the only
   shape. Vary the anchor (brand moment / payout-first / realization
   moment). Don't reuse the situation-list ("in group chats, school
   WhatsApps") verbatim across posts.
4. **CHECK BEFORE SUBMITTING** gains an item #8 — anecdote-template
   self-check.
5. **PROMPT_VERSION** bumped: `2026-05-13.v5` → `2026-05-17.v6`.
6. **`docs/product-thesis.md`** gains a "Rotation discipline" paragraph
   in the Content formats section. Voice unchanged.

**No BLOCKLIST additions.** The patterns found are rotation/template
issues, not dead phrases. Banning "in group chats, school WhatsApps"
would over-restrict a fine phrase — better handled via prompt guidance
that allows the phrase but discourages verbatim reuse.

**No voice pivot.** v5 voice remains locked.
