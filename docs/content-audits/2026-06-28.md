## 3. Pattern analysis

### 3a. THE finding: content_format collapse

Every one of the 6 fb_published posts this week was tagged `anecdote`. The other four formats — `direct`, `math`, `brand-callout`, `objection-reframe` — were used **zero** times. The thesis names variety-across-formats as the *primary* variation axis ("variety comes from **how** we tell the same news … not from what we say"). The system has collapsed to a single texture.

This is invisible to the existing dedup mechanism: angle_tags vary (`thermos`, `tights`, `chair`, `boots`, `slippers`, `wine`), so the recent-angles list never trips the "don't repeat" check. But the *format* keeps going to `anecdote`.

### 3b. Display rhythm rut (anecdote-internal)

Within the all-anecdote set, 5 of 6 displays follow the identical syntactic pattern:

> **`She [verb] the [item] / [prepositional location].`**

Verbatim:
- She named the thermos / in the lunch line
- She named the tights / before the curtain went up
- She named the rain boots / at the puddle by the gate
- She named the slippers / at the book return slot
- She poured the wine / and three sisters-in-law asked the brand

"She named the [X]" appears verbatim 3 times. Even if the format were appropriate every day, this rhythm would feel xeroxed in the FB feed.

### 3c. Geographic rut

City distribution: Cincinnati ×3, Cleveland ×2, Asheville ×1. Half the week in one Midwest city. The thesis doesn't mandate city variety but a national audience seeing "A regular mom in Cincinnati" three times in seven days starts to notice.

### 3d. Visual rut

`image_bg` distribution: `warm-gradient` ×4, `navy-coral-gradient` ×1, `cream` ×1. `coral` / `navy` / `white-coral-ring` unused all week. Same warm-tone palette four days running thins the feed's visual variety.

### 3e. Tagline overuse

The "Real moms. Real money. Real easy." tagline appears in posts 1, 2, and 4 (3/6 = 50%). The thesis is explicit: *"Use selectively — maybe 1 in 4 posts. Never every post."* 50% violates "selectively."

### 3f. Dead-phrase audit — clean

Zero BLOCKLIST hits across all 6 posts. No `gate-kept`, no `rev share`, no `everyday moms`, no `that's so 2025`, no pain-point openers. The v5 BLOCKLIST is doing its job.

### 3g. Caption length

All captions 150–230 words. Within the 80–300 schema range but clustered at the upper end. Not a problem in isolation; worth watching for "Claude is writing essays where a tight 100-word direct hits harder."

## 4. Decision: prompt tuning warranted

Pattern 3a (format collapse) is the strongest signal a Sunday audit can produce: a primary variation axis has stopped varying. Patterns 3b and 3e compound it. This warrants a prompt tweak — version bump to **`2026-06-28.v6`**.

### Changes shipped in this PR

1. **`lib/social/post-generator.ts`**
   - Add **VARIETY MANDATE** section to `SYSTEM_PROMPT` instructing Claude to actively rotate across the 5 formats and to under-weight `anecdote` if it's been used in the most recent posts.
   - Add an explicit anti-pattern: don't reuse the `"She [verb] the [item] / [prepositional location]"` display rhythm two days running.
   - Tighten the tagline guidance: cap "Real moms. Real money. Real easy." at **roughly 1 in 5 posts**, and never two days in a row.
   - Add an optional `recentContentFormats?: string[]` field to `GeneratorInputs` and surface it in the user prompt when present. Defaults to `[]`, so existing callers continue working; a follow-up route PR can wire it.
   - No additions to `BLOCKLIST` — no new bad phrase actually slipped through. Substring banning of the display rhythm would over-trigger; better to handle in prose.
   - Bump `PROMPT_VERSION` → `"2026-06-28.v6"`.

2. **`docs/product-thesis.md`**
   - Add a short paragraph under "Content formats" naming the variety-discipline expectation explicitly: no single format should dominate a 7-day window.

### What this PR does NOT change

- The locked v5 voice. Untouched.
- The locked vocabulary tables. Untouched.
- The canonical 3-piece message. Untouched.
- The BLOCKLIST. Unchanged (nothing new slipped through).
- The schema, the cron route, or any downstream consumers.

This is a **refinement** of v5, not a pivot.
